## Before Updating
* **Update client and server together.** This version adds new blocks, so a 1.1.0 client cannot join a 1.0.x server or vice versa.
* **New config options** in `config/productivecows-server.toml`: `coolerTankCapacity`, `coolerEnergyCapacity`, `coolerCycleTicks` and `coolerEnergyPerStamp`. Existing values are kept.

## New Features
* **Cooling Tower**, an end-game multiblock that cools many fluids at once. Build a 3x3 tower from 3 to 10 blocks tall:
  * **Cooler Casing** for the frame: all corners, plus the bottom and top layers.
  * **Cooler Controller**, one per tower, with its screen facing out. Right-click opens the GUI; sneak + right-click tells you what is wrong with an unfinished tower, and where.
  * **Cooler Tank**, in the inner layers (up to 8): each one adds 16 B to the tower's single tank, which holds any mix of fluids, shown as stacked layers in the GUI.
  * **Cooler Stamp**, in the centre of each inner layer: each Stamp cools 2 ingots every 5 seconds. A full tower has 8 Stamps and makes 16 ingots at a time, four times a fully upgraded Advanced Cooler. Ingots are shared among the stored fluids in turn.
  * **Cooler Port**: right-click to switch between input (fluids and RF) and output (ingots, pushed into the inventories next to it).
* Right-click the Controller with a filled bucket to pour the fluid straight in.
* The tower runs on RF: each working Stamp uses 60 RF/t, and without power it pauses without losing anything.
* The Cooler Tank has a clear glass window, so you can see into the tower.
* JEI shows how to build the tower on each of its blocks, lists the Controller among the cooling machines and opens the recipes from its progress bar. Jade shows the tower's size, Stamps, Tanks, fluids and progress on the Controller and the Ports.
* **Creative Energy Source**: a creative-only block (no recipe) that gives unlimited RF to everything next to it, for testing and creative builds.
